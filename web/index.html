    BEHIND THE CONVERSATION

    One question. Connected expertise.

    Specialized agents share one consultation state, from context gathering to a grounded response.

    THE ORIGINAL PIPELINE

    Conversation → retrieval → review → local lookup

    LangGraph coordinates the handoffs. FAISS brings back relevant manual excerpts; Neo4j connects products with symptoms and population-specific cautions. The final response combines the reviewed candidate with a regional stock example.
